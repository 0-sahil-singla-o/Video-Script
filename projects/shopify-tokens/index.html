100% INVENTORY INTEGRITY
Picture this,
it is Black Friday.
10 ,000 shoppers smash the
checkout button for the last
remaining hoodie at the exact
same millisecond. Who wins?
Think of your database like
a physical notebook.
To buy an item,
the server has to flip
to that exact row,
erase the number one and
write zero.
To stop two people from
buying it, databases lock the
entire row.
But during a flash sale,
thousands of shoppers queue behind
that single lock,
causing connection timeouts,
spinning wheels and crashed servers.
Here is the genius trick
Shopify uses.
Instead of one shared counter
that says six,
create six separate rows,
one for each individual hoodie.
Like six physical tokens sitting
in a glass bowl.
Now, every checkout query asks
for just one token.
And if another customer is
currently touching token one,
the query doesn't wait in
line.
It immediately skips to token
two,
no queue,
no deadlocks.
Thousands of transactions complete in
parallel
and Shopify never sells that
last hoodie twice.
